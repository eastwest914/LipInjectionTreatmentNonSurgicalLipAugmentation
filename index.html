Frequently Asked Questions

What is Hyaluronic Acid Lip Filler?
Hyaluronic acid is a naturally occurring substance in the body that attracts and retains moisture. When used as a lip filler, it temporarily adds shape, structure, and volume to the lips safely and predictably.
How long do lip augmentation results typically last?
Most hyaluronic acid lip filler treatments last anywhere from 6 to 12 months, depending on your individual metabolism and the specific product used.
Is lip injection treatment painful?
Minimal discomfort is involved. We apply a medical-grade topical numbing cream prior to injection, and most fillers also contain a built-in anesthetic (lidocaine) for a smooth experience.
What is the downtime after getting lip fillers?
Downtime is minimal. You may experience minor swelling or bruising for 24 to 48 hours post-treatment, but most clients resume normal daily activities immediately.
Can lip fillers be dissolved if I change my mind?
Yes, hyaluronic acid fillers can easily be dissolved using a specialized enzyme called hyaluronidase, offering complete peace of mind.
